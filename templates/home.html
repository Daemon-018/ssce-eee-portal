About the department

About EEE Dept.

The Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering was established in the academic year 2006 with an annual intake of 60. The Department was accredited by NBA (National Board of Accreditation, AICTE, Delhi) for 2 years in the year 2013. Fourteen batches of students have graduated so far.

The Department is offering an M.Tech course in Power Electronics since 2013 with an intake of 12. The Department focuses on preparing students for a wide range of careers in the power sector.

Equipping students with a unique, enriching experience of moulding tomorrow's technocrats with high calibre and commitment.

Department Vision

To be a Centre of Eminence in Electrical and Electronics Engineering to produce technically competent professionals.

Department Mission

  • M1: Vibrant learning environment & resources for students' personal and professional growth.
  • M2: Make rural students professionally skilful, foster research, design, development and entrepreneurship.
  • M3: Nurture communication skills, leadership, ethics and entrepreneurship for sustained growth.
  • M4: Mutually beneficial industry & government relationships for sustainable societal solutions.
Programmes

Courses Offered

B.Tech

Electrical & Electronics Engineering

Power systems, Electrical Machines, Power Electronics, Control Systems — generation, transmission and distribution of power along with automation and control of industries.

  • 4-year degree · 8 semesters · 18-week semester system
  • University examinations each semester (JNTU-GV)
  • Intake: 60 (Category A) + 12 (Category B)
M.Tech

Power Electronics

Core EEE disciplines plus electives in the latest developments — training manpower for industry needs, research in specialised areas, and engineering faculty growth.

  • 2-year degree · 4 semesters · second year for project
  • Industry, research and teaching pathways
  • Intake: 9 (Category A) + 3 (Category B)
